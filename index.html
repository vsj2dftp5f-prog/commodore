A loving tribute to the Commodore GL-997R (1975). Type on your keyboard or click the keys.
✦ Secrets: press = on a 4-digit number for Kaprekar's routine (→ 6174), or on a perfect number (6, 28, 496, 8128…) to see why it's perfect. Press N to jot a note on your last sum, C to clean the pad & calculator.
✦ S (or ∑ sum) writes a formula on the pad — brackets and % allowed, Enter computes. ✂ tears a block off onto the pinboard — or hold a block to pick it up: drop it on the cork, or shuffle the sheet's order. Post-its slide freely around the board. ✕ next to ✂ throws one line away (tap twice to be sure). ☑ select vinkt meerdere regels aan — 🗑 gooit ze samen weg, Esc of "klaar" stopt het vinken. ⠿ pakt een regel direct op om hem ergens anders neer te zetten (of houd een regel even vast).
